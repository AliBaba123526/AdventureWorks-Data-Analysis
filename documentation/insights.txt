1. Product Category Performance

- Bikes generated the highest internet sales revenue at approximately 28.32 million, significantly ahead of Accessories and Clothing.
- Accessories sold the most units at 36,092, compared with 15,205 Bikes. This shows that Bikes' revenue leadership is not caused by higher sales volume.
- Bikes had a much higher average unit price at approximately 1,862, helping explain why the category generates substantially more revenue despite selling fewer units than Accessories.
- Bikes also generated the highest total profit at approximately 11.51 million.
- Accessories had the highest profit margin at 62.59%, compared with 40.63% for Bikes and 40.14% for Clothing.
- Overall, Bikes are the main driver of absolute revenue and profit, while Accessories perform particularly strongly in unit sales and profit-margin efficiency.

2. Sales Trend Analysis

The dataset contains sales from 29 December 2010 to 28 January 2014, meaning 2010 and 2014 are only partial years and should not be used for fair full-year comparisons.

For the complete years, revenue fell from approximately 7.08 million in 2011 to 5.84 million in 2012, before increasing sharply to approximately 16.35 million in 2013.

The 2012 decline appears across several months rather than being caused by one isolated weak month.

2013 shows sustained improvement across the year, with monthly revenue frequently exceeding 1 million.

December 2013 was the strongest month in the full-year comparison, generating approximately 1.87 million in revenue.

The strong finish to 2013 suggests positive sales momentum, although 2014 is incomplete and should not yet be interpreted as a full-year decline.

 3. Geography Analysis

- Australia was the highest-performing individual sales territory, generating approximately 9.06 million in revenue.
- However, when all United States territories were combined at country level, the United States generated the highest total revenue at approximately 9.39 million, narrowly ahead of Australia.
- The United States also generated the highest total profit at approximately 3.90 million, followed by Australia at approximately 3.69 million.
- Canada had the highest profit margin at 41.96%, followed closely by the United States at 41.54%.
- Australia ranked second for total revenue and profit but had the lowest profit margin at 40.67%.
- Profit margins were relatively similar across all countries, with only a small difference between the highest and lowest margins. This suggests that geography has a stronger effect on total sales volume and revenue than on profit-margin efficiency.
- Overall, the United States is the strongest market by total revenue and profit, while Australia is the strongest individual sales territory.

 4. Customer Analysis

- Nichole Nara generated the highest individual customer revenue at approximately 13,295, although the top customers were very closely grouped.
- The customers placing the most orders were not the same customers generating the most revenue. This shows that purchase frequency alone does not determine customer value.
- Some customers had very high average order values but relatively few orders, while the highest-revenue customers tended to combine reasonable order frequency with relatively high order values.
- Nichole Nara generated the most revenue through a strong combination of order frequency and average order value rather than being the highest on either individual measure.
- Customer revenue is not concentrated around one dominant customer. The top 10 customers contributed only approximately 0.44% of total internet sales revenue.
- Overall, customer value is driven by a combination of purchase frequency and order value, while the low top-10 revenue concentration suggests Adventure Works has a broadly distributed customer revenue base.
